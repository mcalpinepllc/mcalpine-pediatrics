# Brand and Experience Direction

## Three Stylistic Approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| **The Open Porch** | Quiet Southern hospitality translated into warm daylight, graceful editorial type, and the easy reassurance of being welcomed by someone who knows your family. | 0.037 |
| **Coral Storybook Modernism** | A brighter, more illustrative direction in which pediatric optimism meets mid-century shapes, expressive color, and childlike discovery without becoming juvenile. | 0.081 |
| **Savannah Garden Clinic** | A botanical, restorative identity inspired by shaded squares, live oaks, soft plaster, and the cultivated calm of an intimate neighborhood practice. | 0.024 |

## Chosen Approach: The Open Porch

**The Open Porch** is the selected direction. It treats the practice less like a conventional medical office and more like a trusted front porch: gracious, calm, intergenerational, and immediately useful. The visual system must feel established enough to honor Dr. McAlpine’s pioneering career while remaining bright and effortless for today’s parents.

### Design Movement

The movement is **Southern Editorial Modernism**: the restraint and typographic confidence of a contemporary editorial journal softened by Savannah light, vernacular porch architecture, hand-drawn botanical gestures, and welcoming domestic details. It is sophisticated rather than clinical and warm rather than whimsical.

### Core Principles

| Principle | Design Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| **Reassurance before information** | Every screen should answer “Am I in caring, capable hands?” before asking a parent to process details. |
| **One clear next step** | Scheduling, calling, and checking coverage are always obvious, with no competing clusters of buttons. |
| **Earned warmth** | Coral, family imagery, and conversational language are used with restraint; credibility comes from precise facts and calm structure. |
| **Dignity across generations** | The site honors a historic career without feeling like an archive and serves caregivers without infantilizing them. |

### Color Philosophy

The signature color is **McAlpine Coral** (`#E96853`), a sun-warmed coral chosen for human warmth, vitality, and visibility. It is balanced with **Porch Cream** (`#FFF9F1`), **Live Oak** (`#173F3A`), **Sage Shade** (`#DDE9DF`), and **Ink Brown** (`#302A27`). Coral should guide action and affection; deep green carries authority and contrast; cream keeps the experience luminous and domestic. No purple gradients or medical-blue defaults are permitted.

### Layout Paradigm

The site follows a **porch-to-parlor sequence** rather than a centered grid. The opening is an asymmetric editorial spread: a large photographic field and an offset content panel with an immediate appointment route. Subsequent sections alternate between generous narrative bands and compact utility stations. Thin rules, offset labels, and margin notes create rhythm without adding clutter. On mobile, the sequence becomes a single clear path with sticky access to booking.

### Signature Elements

The identity uses three recurring motifs: a **coral doorway arch** framing selected images, a **single hand-drawn live-oak branch line** that appears as a quiet divider, and a **small heart-and-sprout emblem** suggesting care that helps children grow. These elements must remain sparse enough to feel ownable rather than decorative.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like a gracious handoff. Hover states deepen coral toward brick and lift elements by only a few pixels. Appointment selection reveals one decision at a time. Insurance checking returns plain-language guidance without implying a guarantee of coverage. Keyboard focus is obvious, error messages are specific, and every control has a generous touch target.

### Animation

Motion is limited to short 160–240 ms transitions using a confident ease-out. Editorial sections may rise eight pixels and fade in once as they enter view, with 50 ms stagger between related elements. Buttons compress to 97% on press. The coral arch may shift subtly behind photography, but all nonessential movement is disabled when `prefers-reduced-motion` is enabled. Nothing loops, bounces, or delays access to information.

### Typography System

Headlines use **Fraunces**, with its soft, cultivated serif forms and occasional italic emphasis. Body copy, controls, and practical information use **Manrope**, selected for open counters and excellent readability at smaller sizes. Display headings are large but not loud, sentence-cased, and tightly composed. Eyebrows use Manrope in uppercase with expanded letter spacing. Body text never falls below 16px, and practical labels prioritize clarity over ornament.

### Brand Essence

**Savannah’s pioneering pediatric care, offered with the steady expertise of a physician and the generous heart of a community mother.** The brand is **gracious, assured, and devoted**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines sound warm, grounded, and specific. Calls to action use plain verbs and set expectations. Microcopy anticipates the practical questions a parent asks while juggling work, school, transportation, and insurance. Two model lines are: **“Care that knows your child—and remembers your family.”** and **“Choose a time now. We’ll help with the details next.”** Generic welcomes and promotional superlatives are prohibited.

### Wordmark and Logo

The wordmark pairs a custom stacked **“W. Esther”** signature-style serif treatment with a precise small-cap **“McAlpine, M.D., P.C.”** line. The symbol is a coral doorway/heart containing a small sprouting leaf: the doorway expresses welcome, the heart expresses care, and the sprout expresses healthy growth. The symbol must remain bold, text-free, and recognizable at favicon size.

### Signature Brand Color

**McAlpine Coral — `#E96853`** is the ownable color. It should appear on the most important action, the emblem, selected editorial accents, and nowhere so frequently that it loses meaning.

## Style Decisions

Utility areas are treated as **gracious editorial handoffs** using cream paper surfaces, thin rules, offset numbering, margin notes, and restrained containment rather than generic product-card clusters. Generic circular icons are minimized in favor of the practice’s coral doorway/heart mark, italic editorial numbering, live-oak lines, and arch geometry. McAlpine Coral remains concentrated on the emblem, primary actions, slim structural accents, and one appointment moment; Porch Cream, Sage Shade, and Live Oak carry the larger surfaces. Photography is directed toward Savannah light, live-oak shade, family familiarity, and domestic warmth, while any unverified or representational imagery is not presented as an authentic photograph of Dr. McAlpine.
